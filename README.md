# Arcade
Arcade is a console game collection made in C++. It has two minigames in one menu: a quiz that guesses your political leaning and a streaming platform simulator where you grow your own digital empire.
 
![Arcade](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a150ae-0050-702b-a3ae-d6683bcc92d6/image.png)
 
## What does it do? 
- **Political Robot:** Answer 4 quick questions (Yes, No or Don't know) and the robot guesses which political leaning you match the most.
- **Streaming Simulator:** You own a streaming platform. Register Video, Music and Gaming subscriptions. The more income you make, the higher your level: from *Garage Startup* all the way to *Digital Empire*.
 
## How to use it?
1. Go to the Releases section of this repository.
2. Download the .exe file.
3. Game
